SCHEME NAME: National Scholarship for Persons with Disabilities DEPARTMENT: Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment (Central Government) WHO CAN APPLY: All 21 recognized disabilities (minimum 40% disability). Age: School and college students. Income limit: Varies by level (e.g., up to Rs. 2.5 lakh/year family income for pre-matric/post-matric). Check with local office for current limits. WHAT YOU GET: Financial support covering tuition fees, book allowances, and a maintenance allowance to ensure you can complete your education. DOCUMENTS NEEDED:
Disability Certificate (minimum 40%)
Income Certificate
Aadhaar Card
Previous year's mark sheet
Fee receipt of current course WHERE TO APPLY: National Scholarship Portal (NSP) online. HOW TO APPLY:
Go to the National Scholarship Portal (scholarships.gov.in).
Register as a new student using your Aadhaar number.
Fill in the online form and upload scanned copies of your documents.
Submit the application online so your school or college can verify it.
SCHEME NAME: NHFDC Loans for Disabled Persons DEPARTMENT: National Handicapped Finance and Development Corporation (NHFDC) WHO CAN APPLY: All recognized disabilities (minimum 40% disability). Age: 18 years and above. Income limit: No strict income limit, but preference is given to lower-income groups. WHAT YOU GET: Low-interest microfinance, education loans, or business loans to help you become financially independent or fund higher studies. DOCUMENTS NEEDED:
Disability Certificate (minimum 40%)
Aadhaar Card and PAN Card
Project report (for business loans) or admission letter (for education loans)
Guarantor details or collateral documents WHERE TO APPLY: Apply through the state channelizing agency (KSHPWC in Kerala) or partner banks like Punjab National Bank. HOW TO APPLY:
Decide if you need an education loan or a business loan.
Contact KSHPWC or a participating public sector bank.
Fill out the NHFDC loan application form.
Submit the form with your business plan or college admission proof and guarantee documents.
SCHEME NAME: National Trust Schemes (Niramaya Health Insurance) DEPARTMENT: National Trust (Ministry of Social Justice) WHO CAN APPLY: Persons with Autism, Cerebral Palsy, Mental Retardation (Intellectual Disability), and Multiple Disabilities. Age: No age limit. Income limit: No income limit for eligibility, but BPL families pay a lower enrollment fee. WHAT YOU GET: Affordable health insurance cover up to Rs. 1 Lakh per year, covering medical checkups, hospitalization, therapy, and corrective surgeries without requiring pre-medical tests. DOCUMENTS NEEDED:
Disability Certificate (specifically for Autism, CP, MR, or Multiple Disabilities)
Aadhaar Card
BPL Card / Income Certificate (to qualify for the lower fee rate)
Passport size photograph WHERE TO APPLY: Authorized Registered Organizations (RO) or NGOs of the National Trust in your district. HOW TO APPLY:
Find a local NGO that is registered with the National Trust (list available on thentrust.gov.in).
Go to the NGO with your documents and the enrollment fee (usually Rs. 250 for BPL, Rs. 500 for APL).
The NGO will apply on the National Trust online portal on your behalf.
You will receive a Niramaya Health Insurance e-card to use at hospitals.
