SCHEME NAME: Post-Matric Scholarship for Disabled Students
DEPARTMENT: Social Justice Department (SJD), Kerala
WHO CAN APPLY: All recognized disabilities (minimum 40% disability), studying in Class 11 up to Post-Graduation. Income limit: Check with the local office for current limits.
WHAT YOU GET: Financial assistance to help pay for tuition fees, study materials, and hostel expenses so you can continue your higher education without financial stress.
DOCUMENTS NEEDED:
Disability Certificate (minimum 40%)
Income Certificate from Village Officer
Aadhaar Card
Bank passbook copy
Mark list of the previous qualifying examination
Certificate from the Head of the Educational Institution
WHERE TO APPLY: Nearest District Social Justice Office or apply through the Head of your Institution.
HOW TO APPLY:
Get the application form from the Social Justice Office or your college/school.
Fill out the form and attach all required documents.
Submit the form to the Head of your Educational Institution.
The institution will forward it to the District Social Justice Officer for approval.
SCHEME NAME: Pre-Matric Scholarship for Disabled Students
DEPARTMENT: Social Justice Department (SJD), Kerala
WHO CAN APPLY: All recognized disabilities (minimum 40% disability), students studying from Class 1 to Class 10. Income limit: Check with local office for current limits.
WHAT YOU GET: A regular monthly scholarship amount to help cover the costs of basic schooling, textbooks, and uniforms.
DOCUMENTS NEEDED:
Disability Certificate (minimum 40%)
Income Certificate
Aadhaar Card
Bank passbook copy
Certificate from the School Principal/Headmaster
WHERE TO APPLY: Nearest District Social Justice Office or apply through your school.
HOW TO APPLY:
Collect the application form from your school or local Social Justice Office.
Fill the application and attach copies of your documents.
Submit it to your school's Headmaster or Principal.
The school will send it to the District Social Justice Office for processing.
SCHEME NAME: State Disability Pension (Vikalanga Pension)
DEPARTMENT: Social Justice Department (SJD) / Kerala Social Security Mission
WHO CAN APPLY: All recognized disabilities (minimum 40% disability). Age: No age limit (applies to children, adults, and the elderly). Income limit: Annual family income must be below Rs. 1,00,000.
WHAT YOU GET: A regular monthly pension of Rs. 1,600 credited directly to your bank account. Persons with 75% or more disability may get an extra caregiver allowance of Rs. 1,000 per month.
DOCUMENTS NEEDED:
Disability Certificate from a Government Medical Board (minimum 40%)
Income Certificate from Village Office
Aadhaar Card
Ration Card
Bank passbook copy
Self-declaration that you are not receiving other government pensions
WHERE TO APPLY: Local Grama Panchayat, Municipality, or Corporation Office.
HOW TO APPLY:
Get the pension application form from your local Panchayat or Municipality office.
Fill the form and attach all required documents.
Submit the form back to the local body office.
The application will be verified by the local Welfare Standing Committee before the pension starts.
SCHEME NAME: Marriage Assistance to Daughters of Differently Abled Parents (Parinayam)
DEPARTMENT: Social Justice Department (SJD), Kerala
WHO CAN APPLY: Differently-abled women, or differently-abled parents conducting the marriage of their daughter. Age: The bride must be 18 years or older. Income limit: Annual family income must not exceed Rs. 1,00,000.
WHAT YOU GET: A one-time financial grant of Rs. 30,000 to help cover marriage expenses.
DOCUMENTS NEEDED:
Disability Identity Card / Certificate
Age proof of the bride (Birth Certificate or SSLC book)
Income Certificate from the Village Officer
Ration Card copy
Marriage invitation card or marriage confirmation details
Bank passbook copy
WHERE TO APPLY: District Social Justice Office or via SUNEETHI Online Portal.
HOW TO APPLY:
Get the application form from the District Social Justice Office or register on the SUNEETHI online portal.
Fill in the details of the bride and the disabled applicant.
Attach the income certificate, disability proof, and marriage details.
Submit the form to the District Social Justice Office for verification.
SCHEME NAME: Artificial Limbs and Appliances Scheme
DEPARTMENT: Social Justice Department (SJD), Kerala
WHO CAN APPLY: Persons with physical or locomotor disabilities (minimum 40% disability). Age: All ages. Income limit: Annual family income must not exceed Rs. 1,00,000.
WHAT YOU GET: Free assistive devices like artificial limbs, wheelchairs, tricycles, crutches, or hearing aids to help you move and live more independently.
DOCUMENTS NEEDED:
Disability Certificate (minimum 40%)
Income Certificate from Village Office
Aadhaar Card
Passport size photograph
Medical certificate stating the applicant can safely use the requested device
WHERE TO APPLY: District Social Justice Office.
HOW TO APPLY:
Collect the form from the District Social Justice Office.
Get a government doctor to certify that you need and can use the specific device (like a wheelchair).
Attach your income and disability certificates.
Submit the complete application to the District Social Justice Officer.
