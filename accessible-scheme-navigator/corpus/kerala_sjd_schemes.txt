SCHEME: Post-Matric Scholarship for Differently Abled Students (SJD Kerala)
FIELD: overview
CONTENT: This is a monthly scholarship from Kerala's Social Justice Department for disabled students studying from Class 11 (+1) up to Degree, Postgraduate, Polytechnic, Professional, and other higher courses, including hostel and day scholars.
FIELD: eligibility
CONTENT: You must have a disability of 40% or more, certified by a Medical Board. Your disability should be Blind, Deaf, Orthopedically Handicapped, or Mental Retardation (as per the department's listed categories). Your combined family annual income must be below Rs. 36,000. You must have scored at least 40% marks in your previous year's exam.
FIELD: benefit
CONTENT: Monthly scholarship amount depends on your course level: +1/+2/IT courses get Rs. 750 (day scholar) or Rs. 1,000 (hostel student); Degree, Polytechnic, and equivalent training courses get Rs. 1,000 (day scholar) or Rs. 1,500 (hostel student); Professional and PG courses get Rs. 1,000 (day scholar) or Rs. 1,500 (hostel student). Blind students also get a Reader's Allowance of Rs. 300–400 per month depending on course level.
FIELD: documents
CONTENT: 1. Filled application form 2. Attested copy of Disability Certificate from a Medical Board 3. Income certificate from the concerned Village Officer 4. Proof of admission/study in the current course
FIELD: next_step
CONTENT: Visit your District Social Justice Office within 3 months of the start of the academic year. Tell the officer, "I want to apply for the Scholarship for Differently Abled Students for my +1/degree/PG course," and ask them for the application form and current income/disability certificate requirements.

SCHEME: Pre-Matric Scholarship for Differently Abled Students (SJD Kerala)
FIELD: overview
CONTENT: This is a monthly scholarship from Kerala's Social Justice Department for disabled students studying from Class 1 to Class 10, meant to support their school education.
FIELD: eligibility
CONTENT: You must have a disability of 40% or more, certified by a Medical Board. Your disability should be Blind, Deaf, Orthopedically Handicapped, or Mental Retardation (as per the department's listed categories). Your combined family annual income must be below Rs. 36,000. You must have scored at least 40% marks in your previous year's exam.
FIELD: benefit
CONTENT: Monthly scholarship amount depends on your class: Class 1 to 4 students get Rs. 300 per month; Class 5 to 10 students get Rs. 500 per month. Blind students in these classes also get a Reader's Allowance of Rs. 200 per month.
FIELD: documents
CONTENT: 1. Filled application form 2. Attested copy of Disability Certificate from a Medical Board 3. Income certificate from the concerned Village Officer 4. School admission or study proof for the current year
FIELD: next_step
CONTENT: Visit your District Social Justice Office within 3 months of the start of the academic year. Tell the officer, "I want to apply for the Scholarship for Differently Abled Students for my child in Class [X]," and ask them for the application form and current documentation requirements.

SCHEME: State Disability Pension - Vikalanga Pension (Indira Gandhi National Disability Pension Scheme)
FIELD: overview
CONTENT: This is a monthly cash pension for people with disabilities in Kerala. It is actually implemented by Local Self-Government bodies (Panchayats, Municipalities, Corporations), not directly by the Social Justice Department, though it is commonly grouped with SJD welfare schemes and referred to locally as "Vikalanga Pension."
FIELD: eligibility
CONTENT: You must have a disability of 40% or more, certified by a Medical Board. Your combined family annual income must be Rs. 1,00,000 or below. You should not already be receiving any other government social welfare pension. You should not own a car or similar taxable vehicle, and should not be an income tax payer. There is no age limit for this pension.
FIELD: benefit
CONTENT: As of recent years, the pension amount is Rs. 1,600 per month for all Kerala social security pension categories including disability pension. People with 75% or more disability, or who are bedridden or need constant care, may get an additional caregiver allowance of around Rs. 1,000 per month. Please confirm the exact current amount at your local office, since pension amounts are revised by government order from time to time.
FIELD: documents
CONTENT: 1. Filled pension application form 2. Disability certificate from a government Medical Board (or UDID card) 3. Aadhaar card 4. Income certificate from the Village Officer 5. Bank or post office passbook copy 6. Ration card or residence proof 7. Passport-size photograph
FIELD: next_step
CONTENT: Visit your local Grama Panchayat, Municipality, or Corporation office (wherever you live). Ask for the Secretary or the pension section and say, "I want to apply for the Indira Gandhi National Disability Pension (Vikalanga Pension)," and ask them for the application form.

SCHEME: Marriage Assistance - Parinayam (SJD Kerala)
FIELD: overview
CONTENT: Parinayam is a Kerala Social Justice Department scheme that gives one-time financial help for the marriage of disabled women, or for the marriage of daughters of disabled parents.
FIELD: eligibility
CONTENT: Combined annual family income should not exceed Rs. 1,00,000. The bride must have completed 18 years of age at the time of applying. Assistance can be given for the marriage of up to 2 daughters in a family, but the application for the second daughter's marriage can only be made 3 years after the first grant was given.
FIELD: benefit
CONTENT: A one-time payment of Rs. 30,000 is given to the eligible beneficiary.
FIELD: documents
CONTENT: 1. Attested copy of Ration Card 2. Disability Identity Card 3. Birth Certificate (from Corporation/Municipality/Panchayat) showing the bride is the daughter of a disabled person, or the bride's own disability certificate if she is the disabled person 4. Family Income Certificate from the concerned Village Officer
FIELD: next_step
CONTENT: Visit your District Social Justice Office. Tell the officer, "I want to apply for Parinayam marriage assistance," and collect the application form from that office; you also submit the completed form with documents there.

SCHEME: Artificial Limbs and Appliances Scheme (SJD Kerala)
FIELD: overview
CONTENT: This Kerala Social Justice Department scheme provides free assistive devices, such as wheelchairs, smartphones with screen readers, Daisy players, CP wheelchairs, and talking calculators, to disabled persons to help with mobility, communication, and daily activities.
FIELD: eligibility
CONTENT: You must have a disability of 40% or more. Combined annual family income must not exceed Rs. 1,00,000. You must not have already received the same or a similar assistive device from any other government or non-government organisation. A Medical Board must confirm that you are capable of using the device you are applying for.
FIELD: benefit
CONTENT: You receive the assistive device itself (not cash), purchased by the department through open tender or from approved agencies, matched to your specific disability and need.
FIELD: documents
CONTENT: 1. Filled application form 2. Disability certificate from a Medical Board 3. Income certificate from the Village Officer 4. Medical Board recommendation confirming you can use the requested device
FIELD: next_step
CONTENT: Watch for the newspaper or Anganwadi centre announcement inviting applications, or directly visit your District Social Justice Office and say, "I want to apply for the assistive devices scheme for disabled persons," and ask for the application form. Your local Anganwadi worker or Child Development Project Officer can also help you apply.
