SCHEME NAME: ADIP Scheme (Assistance to Disabled Persons for Purchase/Fitting of Aids and Appliances) DEPARTMENT: Kerala State Handicapped Persons Welfare Corporation (KSHPWC) / Central Government WHO CAN APPLY: All recognized disabilities (minimum 40% disability). Age: All ages. Income limit: Monthly income up to Rs. 22,500 for full free subsidy; Rs. 22,501 to Rs. 30,000 for a 50% subsidy. WHAT YOU GET: High-quality, modern assistive devices like motorized tricycles, smart canes, hearing aids, and prosthetics to reduce the impact of your disability. DOCUMENTS NEEDED:
Disability Certificate (minimum 40%)
Income Certificate from Revenue Agency or BPL card
Aadhaar Card
Passport size photographs WHERE TO APPLY: KSHPWC Office or during authorized local ADIP assessment camps. HOW TO APPLY:
Look out for ADIP assessment camps held in your district by KSHPWC or local authorities.
Go to the camp with your documents.
Doctors at the camp will assess what specific device you need.
The device will be distributed to you at a follow-up camp.
SCHEME NAME: Loan Scheme for Self Employment DEPARTMENT: Kerala State Handicapped Persons Welfare Corporation (KSHPWC) WHO CAN APPLY: Persons with 40% to 100% disability who want to start a business and are not government employees. Age: Usually 18 to 55 years. Income limit: Check with KSHPWC for current limits. WHAT YOU GET: A low-interest loan up to Rs. 50 Lakhs to help you start your own business, shop, or self-employment venture. DOCUMENTS NEEDED:
Disability Certificate (40% - 100%)
Aadhaar Card and Ration Card
Project report of the business you want to start
Property document or salary certificate of a government employee as a guarantee
Canara Bank account passbook WHERE TO APPLY: KSHPWC Head Office or download the form from hpwc.kerala.gov.in. HOW TO APPLY:
Download the application form from the KSHPWC website or collect it from their office.
Create a basic project report explaining the business you want to do.
Arrange for a guarantor (property valuation or a government employee's salary certificate).
Submit the form, project report, and guarantee documents to KSHPWC.
SCHEME NAME: Skill Training and Placement Programme DEPARTMENT: Kerala State Handicapped Persons Welfare Corporation (KSHPWC) WHO CAN APPLY: Persons with disabilities (minimum 40% disability) looking for jobs. Age: Youth and working-age adults. Income limit: Not generally applicable for training. WHAT YOU GET: Free vocational training in skills like computer operation, tailoring, or electronics, followed by help finding a job in the private or public sector. DOCUMENTS NEEDED:
Disability Certificate (minimum 40%)
Educational Certificates (SSLC/Degree)
Aadhaar Card
Passport size photographs WHERE TO APPLY: KSHPWC Office or authorized training centers in Kerala. HOW TO APPLY:
Check the KSHPWC website or local newspapers for new training batch announcements.
Apply with your educational and disability certificates.
Attend the interview or counseling session to select a suitable course.
Complete the training to receive placement assistance.
