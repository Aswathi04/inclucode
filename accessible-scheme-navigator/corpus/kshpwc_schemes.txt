SCHEME: ADIP Scheme - Assistive Devices (Central Government, via DEPwD/ALIMCO/State Agencies)
FIELD: overview
CONTENT: ADIP stands for "Assistance to Disabled Persons for Purchase/Fitting of Aids and Appliances." It is a Central Government scheme, running since 1981, that helps disabled persons get modern, durable assistive devices like wheelchairs, hearing aids, artificial limbs, and Braille equipment. KSHPWC is one of the state-level agencies involved in distributing these devices in Kerala.
FIELD: eligibility
CONTENT: You can be an Indian citizen of any age. You must hold a disability certificate showing 40% or more disability (benchmark disability under the RPWD Act). Your monthly income from all sources (or your parents'/guardian's income if you are a dependent) must not exceed Rs. 30,000. You should not have received assistance for the same device in the last 3 years (1 year for children below 12).
FIELD: benefit
CONTENT: You get the assistive device itself, at little or no cost. There are also fixed amounts for essential surgery or medical correction needed before fitting a device, for example around Rs. 1,500 for speech and hearing impairment cases (exact amounts vary by disability type and are revised periodically).
FIELD: documents
CONTENT: 1. Disability certificate (40% or more) or UDID card 2. Aadhaar card 3. Income proof/certificate 4. Passport-size photograph 5. Medical assessment/prescription confirming which device you need
FIELD: next_step
CONTENT: Visit your nearest District Disability Rehabilitation Centre, ALIMCO camp, or KSHPWC office, or register yourself online on the ARJUN Portal (adip.depwd.gov.in). Say, "I want to apply for an assistive device under the ADIP scheme," and ask them to check your eligibility and guide you to the next camp or distribution centre.

SCHEME: Loan Scheme for Self Employment (KSHPWC)
FIELD: overview
CONTENT: KSHPWC (Kerala State Handicapped Persons Welfare Corporation) is the state agency that channels loans from NHFDC (National Divyangjan Finance and Development Corporation) to help persons with disabilities start their own business or self-employment venture.
FIELD: eligibility
CONTENT: You must be a person with disability of 40% to 100%. You must not be a government employee. You need to submit a detailed project report and cost estimate for the business you want to start, along with property or salary documents as loan guarantee.
FIELD: benefit
CONTENT: A loan of up to Rs. 50 lakh can be given for self-employment ventures, generally equal to 100% of the valuation of the guarantee property offered. Interest rates for NHFDC-linked loans are concessional (typically in the range of about 4-9% per year depending on the loan amount and scheme, with an additional rebate for women applicants); please confirm the current rate at the time of application as it can change.
FIELD: documents
CONTENT: 1. Duly filled application form in the prescribed format 2. Medical Board certificate confirming disability 3. Detailed project report and cost estimate for the business 4. Property document or government salary certificate as loan guarantee 5. Valuation certificate of the guarantee property, from the Village Officer and the LSGD PWD Engineer 6. Canara Bank account (required, since disbursement is made through this bank)
FIELD: next_step
CONTENT: Visit the KSHPWC office (head office in Poojappura, Thiruvananthapuram, or your nearest KSHPWC regional office). Say, "I want to apply for the NHFDC self-employment loan through KSHPWC," and ask them for the application form and current interest rate.
