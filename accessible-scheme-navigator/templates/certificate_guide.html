Disability Certificate Guide

Why you need this first

Every government scheme for persons with disabilities requires a disability certificate showing 40% or more disability. Without it, you cannot apply for scholarships, pension, assistive devices, or any other benefit. Get this certificate before applying for anything else.

Step by step

  1. Go to your nearest Government District Hospital or Medical College
  2. Ask for the Medical Board — they assess disability certificates
  3. Carry all your documents (listed below)
  4. A board of doctors will assess your disability and assign a percentage
  5. If 40% or above, the certificate is issued — usually the same day or within a week
  6. Apply for your UDID card online using the certificate at swavlambancard.gov.in

Documents to carry

  1. Aadhaar card
  2. Ration card
  3. Passport size photographs (4 copies)
  4. Any existing medical records related to your disability
  5. Age proof (birth certificate or SSLC book)

What is the UDID card?

The Unique Disability ID (UDID) card is a national identity card for persons with disabilities. It is being made mandatory for many central government schemes. Apply online at swavlambancard.gov.in after you receive your disability certificate. It is free.

📍 Where to go: Your nearest Government District Hospital or Government Medical College. Ask at the reception for the Medical Board or the Disability Certificate counter. No appointment needed in most districts.

If your application is rejected

You can appeal to the State Commissioner for Persons with Disabilities, Kerala. Contact details are available at sjd.kerala.gov.in. You are entitled to a written explanation for any rejection.

⚠️ Important: This guide is accurate as of July 2026. Confirm current procedures at your local District Hospital before visiting.
